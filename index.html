The Human
Behind the Bugs

Hi. I'm Arpit. I study quantum physics and astrophysics because apparently regular physics wasn't confusing enough for me.

I spend most of my time thinking about the universe, building robots that have a personality (mostly chaotic), writing code that technically works, and occasionally spending 3 hours debugging only to discover the problem was a function that simply doesn't exist in Python.

My interests include: quantum mechanics, astrophysics, modern physics, AI/ML, robotics, and staring at compiler errors until they feel bad about themselves.

Currently a student. Eventually: someone who fully understands quantum entanglement. Probably. The jury is still in superposition.
